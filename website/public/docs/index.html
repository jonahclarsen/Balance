Balance Docs

A daily planner. Templates generate each day; checked tasks complete goals.

Install

Mac

macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon.

  1. Download and open the disk image.
  2. Drag Balance to Applications and open it.

Android

  1. On the phone, download the APK.
  2. Open it. Allow installs from your browser when asked.
  3. Allow notifications and exact alarms for on-time reminders.

When a new version is out, tap Update in the banner or in Settings → About. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from Balance; later updates install on their own.

Linux

AppImage and .deb builds appear on Releases when published.

Recovery key

On first launch, Balance shows a recovery key. Save it somewhere private, then type it back to continue.

It unlocks your database from a backup or on a new device. Balance can’t recover it for you.

Today

Pick a day template, then Generate today. Check tasks off as you go.

  • Edit: click a task. Enter adds one; Tab indents.
  • Move: drag the handle, or Alt+↑/↓.
  • Done: click the box, or ⌘D.
  • Other days: the date picker, or Alt+Q/W.
  • Regenerate: ⌘⇧G. Tasks you added or edited stay; untouched generated ones are replaced.
  • Undo: ⌘Z, any time.
  • Copy out: selected tasks copy as plain text and paste back with nesting intact.

Days start at 5 a.m., so late nights count toward the previous day.

Day templates

The Days page holds templates. Each row has options; each option has odds.

  • One option below 100%: appears that often.
  • Several options (±): one is drawn by weight. An empty option means skip.
  • Question (?): asks you at generation instead of rolling. Leave a question's only answer blank to type one; the row becomes the question followed by your answer.
  • Only when no active project: right-click a row to drop it, question included, when a project is active at noon that day.
  • “3 goals”: becomes your three most urgent goals.
  • A list’s name: tick the list icon to expand its items into the day.

Use New day for more templates, such as Weekday and Weekend.

Times and reminders

Click the clock on a task to add a time range. Drag either end in 15-minute steps.

In templates, braces insert times when the day is generated and schedule a notification:

WriteGets
{9am}, {22:15}That time
{9am+90m}10:30 AM
{sunset}, {sunset-1h}Vancouver sunset, offset
{sunset+4h <10pm}Four hours after sunset, no later than 10 PM
{sunset >8pm}Sunset, no earlier than 8 PM

Next

Shows only your next unchecked task. ⌘D completes it.

Goals

A goal has a name, a cadence and matching words. Checking a task that contains a word completes it for that day.

  • Example: Exercise, every 2 days, matches run, swim, yoga. Checking “Run 5k” counts.
  • Single words match whole words. Phrases match anywhere. Case doesn’t matter.
  • Goal Rhythm, under Today, shows each goal’s days and what’s due. Drag a goal onto a task to add its name. Alt+A hides it for 6 hours.
  • Stats charts completions over time.
  • Today and the two days before recalculate; older days are kept as recorded.

Quizzes

Questions you answer regularly: text, number or yes/no. Answers become charts.

A task naming a quiz opens it. Import past data brings in earlier answers. Archive quiz moves it out of the rail and Alt+Q/W; its answers and links keep working.

Projects

Keep long efforts in one list. Mark a project active for one to five days, counted from 7 a.m.: it leads the list and shows in the sidebar and widgets until then. Completed and archived projects keep their dates below.

Lists

Reusable checklists, such as an evening reset or packing list. Items have odds and alternative wordings, like day templates.

  • A task containing a list’s name opens that day’s list.
  • Max caps the list’s expected word count.
  • Ideal time sets a target from first to last check-off.
  • List History (Alt+H) shows each day’s generated lists.

Links

Right-click a task, day, note, paragraph, template, quiz, goal, project, bucket, or prioritizing session and choose Copy link. Right-click navigation buttons for page links. Days, lists, and Buckets also offer Copy review link.

Right-click Genuinely Worth Doing, Possibly Worth Doing, or Idea Afterlife and choose Copy review link to review that bucket.

Paste links into tasks or notes, or open them from another app with Balance installed on macOS or Android. Links use IDs, so renaming keeps them working. The destination must exist in your workspace; a link does not share its contents. Hold Shift while right-clicking for the browser’s text-editing menu.

Notes

Notes not tied to a day. Headings, lists, checklists, quotes and images.

  • ⌘N creates one. Type / for formatting.
  • Click a row’s caret or press ⌘R to hide or show its children.
  • Right-click a note or paragraph and choose Copy link. Paste it into a task, note, or another app to return to it.
  • Paste or drop images. Large ones offer compression first.
  • On desktop, hover a note card and click its split icon to open it on the right. Use IMAX for more room; close the right note with its X.
  • Deleted notes go to the Bin.

Prioritize

Start prioritizing, add items, and give each a number. Higher ranks first. Spread evens out crowded numbers.

Buckets

Capture ideas with Alt+K. They land in Proposition Party.

Sort asks one question per idea: worth the time and attention? Yes offers Genuinely Worth Doing, Possibly Worth Doing, Add to new note (N), which moves the idea and its nested items into a new note, or Cut (X), which puts it on the clipboard to paste anywhere. While a day is being generated, Add to day (D) puts it at the top of that day. Back (B) undoes the previous card. Others go to Idea Afterlife or Trash, which empties after 30 days.

Sync

Sync runs through your own relay server. Everything is encrypted before it leaves the device.

1. Run a relay

The relay is a Cloudflare Worker in the repo. With a Cloudflare account:

cd relay-worker
npx wrangler deploy
npx wrangler secret put RELAY_SECRET

Your server address is the worker URL plus the secret: https://balance-relay.you.workers.dev/your-secret.

2. First device

Settings → Sync across your devices → Set up sync from this device. Enter the address, then Set up sync.

3. Other devices

  1. On the first device: Connect another device. It shows a QR code and pairing code.
  2. On the new device: Connect to an existing setup, the same address, then scan or paste the code.
  3. Connect and replace this planner. The new device’s planner is backed up, then replaced.

The pairing code grants access to your planner. Keep it private.

Encryption

  • On device: the database is SQLCipher-encrypted. Its key comes from your recovery key, kept in the macOS Keychain or Android Keystore.
  • In sync: end-to-end encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305. The relay sees only ciphertext.
  • Widgets: get their own encrypted snapshot, never the database key.
  • Rotate: Settings → Database encryption key (Mac).

Balance is open source; you can read every line.

Backups

Balance keeps the last seven daily encrypted backups.

  • Browse: ⌘⇧P, or Settings → Recovery & diagnostics. Search, then Copy text to bring anything back.
  • Export: Settings → Manual export, as JSON or HTML.
  • Freeze report: after a freeze, reopen and choose Export freeze report. It holds no task text.

Situate

Dolo blocks social and messaging apps. Touch grass blocks AI chats. Modes turn on at morning and evening, or only at evening like Touch grass, and a quiz turns one off until the next. A heads-up can warn you 15 or 5 minutes before one comes on. Entertainment sites get a separate short pass. Each mode has its own color, devices, quizzes and blocked apps and websites.

On Mac, keep Balance running and install the Situate extension in Chrome or Brave for websites and selected Discord threads; Balance walks you through it. On Android, grant notification and app-blocking access. Notification history stays encrypted on the phone, with search, filters and export.

Rules and passes are local to each device. Herdr and Cactus can read only the optional Situate status feed. Setup and privacy details.

Widgets and Siri

  • Mac widgets: small, medium and large, showing progress and next tasks.
  • Android widgets: add from the home screen.
  • Shortcuts (macOS 15+): the Add Task to Balance action files a task under “reminders from siri:”.
  • Siri: make a shortcut named “Add to Balance” containing that action, then say its name.

Settings

  • Color theme: eleven themes, or Random for a different one each day. Screenshots here use Iridescent.
  • Appearance: system, light or dark.
  • Completed item colors and Day completion celebrations.
  • Opening messages: shown while the database opens.
  • Export folder: where exports are saved.
